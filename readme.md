<h2><a class="anchor" id="overview"></a>Overview</h2>

The objective of this project is to analyze Airbnb listing performance, revenue distribution, pricing trends, room type popularity, and booking behavior using structured SQL views and interactive dashboards.

The dashboard helps uncover insights that can assist hosts, property managers, and investors in making data-driven pricing and listing optimization decisions.

---
<h2><a class="anchor" id="business-statement"></a>Business Statement</h2>

- Businesses generate large volumes of sales and customer data, but much of it remains unused due to a lack of proper analysis tools.
- Without clear insights, it becomes difficult for managers to identify sales trends, product performance, and customer purchasing behavior.
- This leads to inefficient decision-making, poor inventory planning, and missed business opportunities.
- The project aims to solve this problem by building an interactive Excel dashboard that analyzes data and provides clear visual insights for better business decisions.

---
<h2><a class="anchor" id="dataset"></a>Dataset</h2>

- CSV file located in /data/ folder.

<h2><a class="anchor" id="objectives"></a>Objectives</h2>

- Analyze total listings and total revenue
- Identify most popular room types
- Compare revenue across different regions
- Understand pricing differences by room type
- Analyze instant booking availability
- Evaluate cancellation policy distribution
- Provide data-driven recommendations for hosts

<h2><a class="anchor" id="key-insights"></a>Key Insights</h2>

- Entire homes/apartments are the most preferred room type by guests.
- Hotel rooms have the highest average price among room categories.
- Certain regions contribute significantly higher revenue compared to others.
- Listings with instant booking enabled tend to attract more customers.
- Cancellation policies significantly influence booking behavior.

---
<h2><a class="anchor" id="business-recommendations"></a>Business Recommendations</h2>

- Hosts should prioritize entire home listings for higher occupancy rates.
- Enable instant booking to improve booking frequency.
- Adjust pricing strategies based on regional demand patterns.
- Offer balanced cancellation policies to attract more bookings while protecting revenue.
